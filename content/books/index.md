A curated list of books that have significantly influenced my perspective on deep learning engineering, habit formation, and philosophy.

---

### Machine Learning & Edge Computing

- **[Practical Deep Learning for Cloud, Mobile and Edge](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/practical-deep-learning/9781492034858/)** — *Anirudh Koul, Siddha Ganju & Meher Kasam (O'Reilly)* ★★★★★  
  An indispensable, production-oriented manual on edge AI. Covers model compression, INT8 quantization, latency profiling, and cross-platform deployment across mobile and embedded hardware.  
  [O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/practical-deep-learning/9781492034858/) · [GitHub Code](https://github.com/PracticalDL/Practical-Deep-Learning-Book)

---

### Habits & Personal Systems

- **[Atomic Habits](https://a.co/d/bSYtvvs)** — *James Clear* ★★★★★  
  The most actionable guide on building sustainable daily systems and behavior change: *"You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."*  
  [Amazon](https://a.co/d/bSYtvvs) · See my notes on [Habit Stacking](/posts/habbit-stacking/) and [Meditation Tracking](/posts/meditation-habit/)

---

### Psychology & Philosophy

- **[The Courage To Be Disliked](https://a.co/d/384qcjP)** — *Ichiro Kishimi & Fumitake Koga* ★★★★★  
  An illuminating dialogue grounded in Adlerian psychology. Explores the separation of tasks, interpersonal freedom, and living intentionally in the present moment rather than being bound by past etiology.  
  [Amazon](https://a.co/d/384qcjP)

- **[ကိုယ်ကျင့်အဘိဓမ္မာ](https://dhammadownload.com/File-Library/Ashin-Janakabhivamsa/Ashin-Janakabhivamsa-KoKyintAbhidhamma.pdf)** — *အရှင်ဇနကာဘိဝံသ (မဟာဂန္ဓာရုံဆရာတော်)* ★★★★★  
  ဗုဒ္ဓဘာသာ၏ နက်နဲသိမ်မွေ့သော အဘိဓမ္မာဒေသနာတော်များ၊ စိတ်စေတသိက်တို့၏ ဖြစ်စဉ်များကို နေ့စဉ်လူမှုဘဝတွင် လက်တွေ့ကျင့်သုံးနိုင်သော ကိုယ်ကျင့်တရားနှင့် စိတ်နေသဘောထားဆိုင်ရာ လမ်းညွှန်ချက်များအဖြစ် ပြုစုတော်မူထားသော လက်ရာမွန်။
