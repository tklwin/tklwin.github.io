I am an ML/AI researcher and data scientist specializing in computer vision, low-resource natural language processing (NLP), and edge AI. I currently serve as a Research Assistant under Dr. Rowell M. Hernandez at [Batangas State University](https://batstateu.edu.ph/) and contribute as a researcher at [MMDT-RISE](https://mmdt-rise.istarvz.com/en/about/).

I graduated with a **Master of Science in Data Science** (General Weighted Average: 1.1250, where 1.0 is highest) from Batangas State University as a recipient of the **IIE Myanmar Student Emergency Support (MSES) Fund** by the Institute of International Education. Prior to that, I completed my Bachelor of Computer Technology at the University of Computer Studies, Mandalay (UCSM).

### Research Focus

My research interests center on developing lightweight and resource-efficient AI models tailored for field conditions and low-resource settings:

- **Edge AI & Model Compression:** Designing compact architectures (such as attention-augmented CNNs) and applying static quantization to run deep learning models offline on consumer mobile devices.
- **Low-Resource NLP:** Researching text segmentation, named entity recognition, and linguistic representation for the Myanmar (Burmese) language.
- **Smart Agriculture:** Building offline-capable diagnostic tools for crop diseases to assist smallholder farming communities without reliable internet connectivity.

### Background

Before focusing on academic research, I spent several years working in systems and network engineering:

- **World Health Organization (WHO Myanmar):** Administered national eHealth infrastructure (DHIS2, OpenMRS) supporting data collection and health indicators for over 1,000 public health users.
- **CESVI Myanmar:** Developed agricultural data pipelines and mobile reporting tools across 130 villages in the Dry Zone, training local farming communities in digital tools.
- **Huawei Technologies:** Supported enterprise cloud and carrier infrastructure projects in Myanmar.
