**Research Paper (Under Revision)** | Co-authored with Prof. Sandhya Avasthi, ABES Engineering College, India | 2025

### Overview

Conducted a comparative study of **7 time-series forecasting models** on a 145,000-article Wikipedia Kaggle dataset, evaluated using RMSE as the primary performance metric.

### Models Compared

- Moving Average
- Exponential Smoothing
- ARMA
- ARIMA
- Auto-ARIMA
- Prophet
- LSTM

### Key Finding

Identified **Moving Average** as the best-performing model — demonstrating that simpler statistical models outperformed deep learning (LSTM) on this task, providing practical model selection insights for large-scale web traffic forecasting.
